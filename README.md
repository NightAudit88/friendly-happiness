# PharmTox Scholarship Desk

A web page for finding and tracking scholarships for ASU's BS in Pharmacology & Toxicology.

- **Scholarships:** a curated list of ASU, toxicology and pharmacology awards, with deadlines marked confirmed or estimated
- **Tracker:** set a status on each scholarship; the summary shows your next deadline
- **My profile:** enter your details once and reuse them in applications
- **Essay drafts:** paste a prompt and get a first draft built only from your profile; rewrite it in your own words
- **Find more:** links to Scholarship Universe, SOT, ASPET, FAFSA and national search sites

The page is `scholarship-helper.html`, published as a private claude.ai artifact.
Deadlines were checked in November 2026. Always confirm the date on the official page before you rely on it.
